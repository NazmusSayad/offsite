---
title: Introduction
description: Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode in your browser, on the same sessions as their CLIs.
---

OffSite runs on your machine and opens your Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode sessions in the browser. It uses the same sessions as the CLIs, so you can start a task in the terminal, check on it from your phone and resume it back in the CLI.

OffSite keeps no history of its own. It reads each session from the provider. The only thing it stores is a message you sent from the browser, until the provider takes it.
